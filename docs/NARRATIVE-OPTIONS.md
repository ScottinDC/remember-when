# Narrative options and the next stage

This scope follows the owner's four supplied briefs: Biographic Arc – Long, Biographic Arc – Short, Mode Types, and Feature Selection. Source documents remain in the owner's Drive; they are not copied into this public repository.

## Available in this branch

The account includes Record a memory, My recordings, and My story & downloads. The Sankey tracks actual parent-question relationships; choosing a narrative mode does not invent links, completion, or facts. Passed questions remain visible and can be revisited. Unanswered starting questions and follow-ups can be regenerated. Answered questions retain their original wording so saved audio does not become mislabeled.

The six mode choices are the five requested focused modes (character study, turning point, heritage thread, tribute, milestone) plus the existing life-story default. Preferences also include scope, perspective, framing, voice/tone and intended audience. They are saved per interview and supplied to the AI for future/regenerated questions. Existing questions and recordings are not rewritten automatically.

The short arc displays Baseline, Crucible and Aftermath. The full life arc displays roots, formative years, trial, achievement, reinvention and legacy. Snapshot and medium formats use the focused arc as a flexible guide; the brief does not prescribe a separate stage model for these sizes. A milestone mode is offered with general guidance; the source lists that mode but supplies detailed prompt sets only for the other four.

| Scope | Audio input estimate | Future edited words | Reading estimate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Snapshot | 2–3 minutes | 250–500 | 1–2 minutes |
| Short arc | 6–10 minutes | 800–1,500 | 4–6 minutes |
| Medium arc | 15–25 minutes | 2,500–4,000 | 10–15 minutes |
| Full life arc | 45–90+ minutes over sessions | 6,000–12,000+ | 25–45+ minutes |

These are planning estimates from the brief. Current exports preserve original recordings/transcripts; they do not pad, summarize, rewrite or promise these word counts. Each recording remains at most five minutes; a longer story consists of multiple recordings.

The download workspace supports selection and manual ordering, one combined MP3 of the original voice, a real Word document (.docx), and plain text (.txt). Word/text optionally include questions, retain the transcript wording, and explicitly mark missing transcripts. MP3 conversion runs locally in a worker, downmixes to mono at 128 kbps, and downloads each authorized clip separately before encoding. It never substitutes synthetic speech for the narrator. Per-export limit: three hours; device/browser memory and audio codec support can impose lower practical limits. A failed clip stops the export rather than silently omitting it. Individual original-format downloads remain available.

My recordings is a chronological audio stream with transcript search, playback, download, replacement takes, earlier versions, archive/restore, AI retry and confirmed permanent deletion. Permanent deletion removes that answer's stored audio versions and transcript. The question and already-generated follow-up questions remain for navigation and a new take. Deletion is resumable after a partial failure and locks new playback/processing while pending. Already downloaded files, old emails, provider-side processing records, and retained backups are outside this deletion operation.

## Next stage from the briefs

- A separate editable narrative draft generated only from selected, attributed memories, with the chosen voice, perspective, audience and structure. Preserve sources; never fill gaps with invented facts. Let the narrator review and correct it before exporting.
- More than one named story project per account, with independent selections and narrative choices. The current account uses one interview with selectable subsets for export.
- Photo-assisted prompts and text/rough-notes intake, preserving source provenance and explicit consent for external AI processing.
- A polished reading view, photo placement and print-ready PDF booklet.
- Synchronized transcript/audio playback. Current playback and downloads do not include word-level timing or a rewritten audiobook.
- Long-form generation and exports as durable background jobs for very large archives and less capable devices.

Narrative stages are optional guidance. Real lives may loop, skip stages or end without resolution; the app must not force a crisis or an achievement narrative.
